### Bug fixes

- `evaluate` now counts the labels that a maintainer, or their coding agent, set as they opened their own issue, such as with `gh issue create --label bug`: they are the maintainers' choice, so the replay no longer sees them and the AI's answer is scored against them. Until now such an issue counted as one whose only labels came with its form, so `evaluate` left out almost every issue of a repository whose maintainer labels his own issues. The labels of the issue form that an issue was opened with still count for nothing: `evaluate` finds that form among `.github/ISSUE_TEMPLATE/` of the checkout by the first field that the text begins with, so it reads YAML with PyYAML or `yq` now, as `check` does.
