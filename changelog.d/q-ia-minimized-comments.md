### Bug fixes

- The AI no longer reads a comment that GitHub shows collapsed, as spam, off-topic, outdated or for another reason, whose text the page shows only after a click; that holds for the comments and replies of a discussion as well. The issue that the AI reads keeps the comment's heading, so the numbers of the comments stay those of the page, with a note in place of its text. No file of such a comment is downloaded as an attachment, a reply to it isn't judged, it chooses no language of the comments, the assistant follows up on no questions of its own comment that is collapsed, and a collapsed comment of someone who isn't a maintainer counts as no objection to a duplicate in what later analyses learn. What closes, locks or reopens an issue counts a collapsed comment as before, so collapsing the reporter's answer or an objection never closes an issue, and a maintainer's collapsed comment still keeps the AI out. The log counts the collapsed comments that were left out.
