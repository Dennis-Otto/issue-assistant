### Added

- A summary for the maintainers in their own language when the reporter writes in another one. An analysis, a follow-up or a first answer in Discussions that the AI writes in Japanese, Portuguese or any other language than the maintainers' now ends with a folded block, such as *For the maintainers, in English*: the key facts of the issue in a faithful translation and the gist of the answer, cleaned as every text of the AI. So a maintainer can triage the issue and check what the AI claims without a translator, inside the one comment that the assistant posts. The reporter sees one short, labelled line. A comment in the maintainers' own language has no such block, nor has one on a possible vulnerability. The maintainers read English unless `[maintainers] language` of `config.toml` names another catalog, such as `language = "de"`; `check` fails on a code without a catalog, such as `pt-BR`, and names the one that was probably meant.
