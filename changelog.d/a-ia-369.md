### Bug fixes

- The short excerpts of other issues and discussions that the AI searches for duplicates and related issues keep every word of the reporter. The text above the first field of an issue form, such as an error message, no longer goes; a field that comes twice keeps each of its answers; and a `###` line in a code block, such as a pasted changelog, stays part of the excerpt's text instead of being read as a field of a form. The labels and the opt-out box of a form read every heading as before, so a code block that a reporter leaves open never hides the box.
