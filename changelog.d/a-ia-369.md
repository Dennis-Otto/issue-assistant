### Bug fixes

- The short excerpts of other issues and discussions that the AI searches for duplicates and related issues keep every word of the reporter. The text above the first field of an issue form, such as an error message, no longer goes; a field that comes twice keeps each of its answers; and a `###` line in a code block, such as a pasted changelog, stays part of the text instead of being read as a field of a form, which also no longer counts for the labels and the opt-out box of a form.
