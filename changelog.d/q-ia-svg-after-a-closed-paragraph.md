### Bug fixes

- The AI no longer reads what a line break, a rule, an image or a `<source>` inside `<svg>` or `<math>` holds past the end of its paragraph. Where a tag before the drawing closes the paragraph, such as `<div>`, `<hr>`, `</p>` or the `</h1>` of a heading, GitHub keeps the drawing open past the paragraph, and the page shows nothing after such a tag in it: `x <div><svg><br>`, a blank row and `more words` show only `x`. The assistant now leaves that text out of the issue, its comments, the excerpts and the attachments, and the search for older issues, the language of the comments, the area and whether a pull request denies that it fixes an issue read it the same way. On the safe side, the AI also misses some text that GitHub still shows there, such as after an end tag that closes nothing, in a cell of a table, and after the end of a quote around the drawing.
- A row whose `#` is indented by four columns or a tab no longer counts as a heading where it goes on the paragraph above it, as on GitHub, so what a `<br>` inside `<svg>` holds on it runs to the end of that paragraph.
