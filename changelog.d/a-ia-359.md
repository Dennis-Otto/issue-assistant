### Bug fixes

- The comment that closes an issue with a release promises only what happens on that issue. It names the days of `reopen_after_release_days` in which a comment of the reporter reopens it, 30 by default. On a muted issue, where no AI reads such a comment, it no longer says that the issue reopens: it asks the reporter to write for the maintainers. Their comment there, also long after the release, now gives the issue `needs-triage`, so that the digest lists it under *Waiting on the maintainers* and the sweep doesn't lock it until a maintainer answers. Before, it reached the maintainers only as a notification of a closed issue. The new text is in all fourteen languages.
