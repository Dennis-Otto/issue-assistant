### Bug fixes

- A reporter's comment on an issue that the assistant closed as a duplicate now reaches the maintainers, as the closing comment promises. The issue gets `needs-triage` and stays closed, since whether it is a duplicate is the maintainers' call; the digest lists it under *Waiting on the maintainers*, and the sweep doesn't lock it, until a maintainer answers in a comment. Before, the comment went unnoticed unless a maintainer happened to read GitHub's notification of it.
