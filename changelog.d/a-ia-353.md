### Features

- `install` and `check` now notice a repository that neither Dependabot nor Renovate looks after, whose pins would stay on the release of its set-up and never get a fix: `install` names the step in its last line, and `check` warns, without failing, when there is no `.github/dependabot.yml` with the ecosystem `github-actions` and no configuration of Renovate. *Get started* lists the update tool among the requirements and has a new section, [Turn on updates](https://dennis-otto.github.io/issue-assistant/getting-started.html#turn-on-updates), with the few lines for Dependabot.
