### Bug fixes

- A pull request that describes the bug with a negation before it names the fix, such as `The cache didn't refresh and this fixes #12` or `So it doesn't crash anymore - fixes #12`, now fixes the issue: it gets `fixed-in-next-release` and closes with the release. Before, any *not*, *never* or *n't* among the four words before the keyword counted as words against the fix, and the issue stayed open for good. Now only a negation right before the keyword, or with nothing but a filler such as *yet*, *fully* or *on its own* in between, keeps the issue open, and the summary of the sweep names each issue that stays open so.
