### Bug fixes

- The comments that the assistant writes without the AI, such as the reminder, the closing notice and the notes of a duplicate, a fix or a release, guess the reporter's language better before the first analysis. A Spanish report is no longer taken for Italian, and a short report of one sentence in French, German, Dutch or Italian no longer gets its comments in English: the words that two languages share, such as the "una" of Spanish and Italian, count for neither, each catalog knows more frequent words of its language, and letters that only one language writes count for it, such as ñ for Spanish, ß for German, ã for Portuguese, œ for French and ò for Italian.
