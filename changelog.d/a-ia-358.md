### Bug fixes

- A reporter who adds the missing answer by editing the text of an issue that closed for a missing answer now gets it reopened, as the questions and the closing comment promise: the issue reopens with `needs-triage`, and the assistant follows up on the answer. Before, only a comment reopened it, and the edit went unnoticed, also by the digest, which lists open issues only. A new title or a click on the box of the form alone still reopens nothing.
