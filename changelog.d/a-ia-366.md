### Features

- The daily digest lists the questions in Discussions that wait on the maintainers, where `[discussions] answer_categories` names a category: an open question without a marked answer whose last word is not a maintainer's, at once when the asker or someone else wrote last, such as a reply that the first answer didn't help, and after 3 days when nobody answered or nobody marked the assistant's first answer. Before, such a question appeared nowhere, since discussions have no labels, reminders or closings. The monthly quality numbers count the first answers of the 30 days as well: how many the asker marked as the answer, and their 👍 and 👎.
