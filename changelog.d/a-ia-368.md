### Bug fixes

- The check that keeps tokens and keys out of every comment now knows more kinds of them. A key of the Google APIs, the kind of key that the Gemini engine holds, and the tokens of GitLab, Stripe, Hugging Face and Telegram bots refuse the answer wherever they are, as the tokens of GitHub, Anthropic and OpenAI did. So do long runs of one case and digits, which slipped past before: 64 or more characters of hex and 40 or more of Base32 or of lower case and digits. A commit, the fingerprint of a signing key and a digest that names its algorithm, such as `sha256:` in the digest of an image or `SHA-256: ` before the checksum of a release, still pass; a digest without its algorithm now refuses the answer, since nothing tells it apart from a key.
