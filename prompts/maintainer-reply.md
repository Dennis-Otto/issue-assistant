## Your task: does issue #{issue} now wait for its reporter?

The maintainer just commented on issue #{issue}; the newest comment in `{context}/issue.md` is theirs. Read the issue and its comments.

`waiting_for_reporter` is true only when the maintainer's newest comment asks the reporter, or the people affected, for information, a test, a confirmation or another action, and progress depends on the answer. It is false for announcements, explanations, thanks, plans, answers without a question, questions to other people and rhetorical questions.

`reason` explains the decision in one English sentence. Don't investigate in the repository for this task.
